# Research to Decision Map

| Research capability | Decision question | Production consumer | Role | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fundamentals/financial statements | Is the long-horizon thesis supported by reported economics? | Fair value, readiness, investment recommendation | Core thesis evidence | Keep and validate periods/units |
| Price/liquidity | Can the security be traded and at what execution risk? | Readiness, liquidity overrides, allocation | Eligibility and execution | Keep |
| Technical/SMC | Is there a usable entry structure? | Execution plan and timing | Secondary/execution only | Keep bounded |
| Market regime/macro | What is the portfolio risk posture? | Macro overlay and country-risk assessment | Context and exposure constraint | Keep secondary |
| Corporate events/news | Did a material fact change the thesis? | Catalysts, contradictions, monitoring | Thesis update sensor | Keep with impact gate |
| Cross-stock network | Is relative strength/rotation measurable? | Research-only graph and future relative ranking | Exploratory sensor | Keep isolated until validated |
| Hypothesis/genome | Which research question should be tested? | Methodology and experiment lineage | Research governance | Keep outside direct signal path |
| Capital allocation | Which eligible opportunity is preferable to cash/alternatives? | Portfolio dashboard | Relative allocation | Keep as final allocation layer |

No module should be promoted to the core decision path without declaring its question, input data, output schema, uncertainty, freshness, validation status, downstream consumer, and failure mode.
