# Risk Register

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| R-01 | Conclusions drawn from placeholder data being mistaken for real research output. | High (until a vendor is licensed) | Critical | Placeholder status stamped in code/docs at every placeholder (universe, sectors, holidays, mock CSVs); `PHASE_STATUS.md` states it as the gating item; no external publication of outputs before real data. |
| R-02 | Statistical gates passing spurious relationships at current tiny sample sizes. | High | High | Small-sample adversarial attack + sample-size reviewer thresholds are strict by default (permissive settings exist only inside tests, clearly labeled); confidence capped at 0.9 and adversarially reduced. |
| R-03 | Threshold/constant miscalibration (conflict policy, retirement policy, ontology horizons). | Medium | Medium | All constants centralized, documented as uncalibrated defaults (TD-6), deterministic → recalibration is a config change with replayable comparison. |
| R-04 | JSON store corruption or loss. | Low | High | Integrity-checked backup/verify/restore (refuses partial restores); append-only revisions make partial recovery meaningful. |
| R-05 | Silent divergence between Python schemas and TS mirrors. | Low | Medium | `contracts/` drift check fails CI. |
| R-06 | Vendor lock-in / feed outage once a real provider is integrated. | Medium | Medium | `FallbackDataProvider` composition seam; provider-shape assumptions confined to `data/`. |
| R-07 | Look-ahead bias reintroduced by a future contributor bypassing snapshots. | Low | Critical | Snapshot-only consumption enforced in interfaces; adversarial LookAheadBias attack audits snapshots independently (defense in depth); CLAUDE.md invariants. |
| R-08 | Knowledge base ossifying (nothing retired, stale knowledge driving recommendations). | Medium | High | ContinuousLearningMonitor wired into operations (CLI/runtime); retirement is mechanical, not discretionary; monitoring status transitions are automatic on first evaluation. |
| R-09 | Single-maintainer bus factor on architecture intent. | Medium | Medium | CLAUDE.md + ADR + design docs kept synchronized with code by convention; every invariant also enforced by at least one test. |
| R-10 | Regulatory/legal constraints on investment research publication in Egypt. | Unknown | High | Flagged as a business/legal question for the user before any external distribution of recommendations (out of engineering scope). |
| R-11 | A future contributor adds a collector that bypasses robots.txt, ToS, or authentication, or scrapes HTML where a structured source exists. | Low | High | `Collector.__init__` hard-enforces `IMPLEMENTED`-only status; `HttpFetcher` enforces robots.txt in code; `AccessMethod.HTML_SCRAPE` is documented as last-resort only; every `NEEDS_KEY`/`TOS_REVIEW` source is catalogued but not collectable until the user clears it. |
| R-12 | Real collected data silently mixed with placeholder mock CSVs in the same `LocalCsvDataProvider` layout, with no way to tell which is which. | Medium | Medium | Every collected value traces back to a `RawDocument` (source, license, content hash, fetch time) *and* now a per-value `ProvenanceRecord` (`collectors.provenance_index`) keyed by exactly the (ticker/series, date) cell it produced; placeholder fixtures are never routed through `CollectionService`, so provenance absence itself is the tell. Closed (was previously open pending a materialized-data provenance index). |
| R-13 | A discovered source (`discovery.DiscoveryEngine`) turns out to be malicious, spoofed, or simply wrong, and gets treated as trustworthy before anyone reviews it. | Low | Medium | `DiscoveryEngine` has no import of `SourceRegistry`/`SourceSpec` and cannot register anything itself; `qualification.register_candidate` always mints a new source at `LifecycleState.CANDIDATE`/`SourceStatus.PLANNED` with conservative below-default reliability/freshness priors regardless of caller input; promotion past Candidate requires `evaluate_promotion`'s evidence gates (run history + composite reputation), never a one-off judgment call. |
| R-14 | New third-party dependencies (`pypdf`, `openpyxl`) added for the PDF/Excel collector frameworks introduce supply-chain surface. | Low | Low | Both are widely-used, actively-maintained, permissively-licensed pure/near-pure-Python libraries with no native extensions pulling in further binaries; pinned via `uv`'s lockfile. |
| R-15 | An autonomously-discovered source turns out illegal to collect (ToS actually prohibits it despite passing the heuristic keyword scan) or unreliable, and gets treated as more legitimate than a `TOS_REVIEW`-flagged one because it was "verified" by the engine. | Low | Medium | `legality.assess_legality` never elevates `HTML_SCRAPE` to `ALLOWED` and treats any red-flag/unknown-robots signal as `AMBIGUOUS` (blocked); a generated spec's `status` always stays `PLANNED` regardless of score (`AD-24`), `conflict_priority` is seeded at 40 — below every hand-verified source — so it never outranks a human-reviewed entry in a dispute; becoming collectable still requires an engineer to write and test a real collector. |
| R-16 | The Acquisition Intelligence Engine's domain resolver probes name-derived candidate domains (e.g. `orgname.com`, `orgname.gov.eg`) that don't belong to the intended organization at all (a coincidental registration), and the engine proceeds against the wrong entity's site. | Low | Low | Every probe result requires actual reachability (never assumed); the generated `SourceSpec` records the exact discovered URL and discovery method in `notes` for human review before any engineer wires a real collector against it; the spec never reaches `IMPLEMENTED` without that review. |
| R-17 | The Production Pipeline's per-stage failure isolation lets execution complete "successfully" (`StageStatus.SUCCEEDED`/`PARTIAL`) even when a downstream stage silently produced nothing (e.g. Market Memory fails, so Research Pipeline finds no data and reports 0 hypotheses) — a systemic bug could look like an unremarkable quiet day. | Medium | Medium | Every stage's exact status and detail is recorded in `execution_report.json` (never just a top-line pass/fail); `StageStatus.PARTIAL`/`FAILED` are distinct from `SUCCEEDED` specifically so a degraded run is visible in `mission_status.json`/`report.errors`, not indistinguishable from a healthy one; `docs/TECHNICAL_DEBT.md` TD-25/TD-26 name where stages are checkpoints vs. mutations so a reviewer knows what "succeeded" actually verified. |
| R-18 | `production.pipeline` artifacts (`investment_cases.json`, mock/replay collector output, etc.) get mistaken for real trading signals or real collected market data if published/shared without the same "placeholder until a licensed vendor exists" framing the rest of the platform carries. | Medium | High | Same mitigation as R-01, extended: `collector_status.json`/`execution_report.json` record `execution_mode` (`mock`/`replay`) explicitly on every artifact set; `docs/PHASE_STATUS.md`/`CURRENT_MISSION.md` state plainly that Collector Execution runs against synthetic fixtures until a live collector (the next mission) exists. |
| R-19 | A future contributor, under pressure to "connect real sources faster," fills `generate_company_ir_targets()`'s `domain_hints` or expands `universe.EGX30_UNIVERSE_PLACEHOLDER`/adds an EGX70 list from training-data recall instead of a verified feed or user-supplied input — reintroducing exactly the fabrication this phase deliberately avoided. | Low | High | `generate_company_ir_targets()` is documented (docstring + `CURRENT_MISSION.md`) to intentionally omit domain hints; `TargetOrganization.domain_hints` defaulting to `[]` is asserted by test (`test_generate_company_ir_targets_produces_one_per_company_with_no_fabricated_hints`); `HeuristicDomainResolver` never trusts an unprobed domain regardless of hint source, so a fabricated hint would still need to pass real reachability — but the correct fix if this is ever attempted is to reject the change, not rely on the resolver alone. |
